01 / Who to contact

Pixel Nook is provided by CodeCastle AS, Norway. Contact us about privacy or your data at kingdom@codecastle.ai.

This policy covers the Pixel Nook website and its installed iOS and Android apps. Advertising features described below apply where they are enabled in the installed app.

02 / Saved progress

The game stores local highscores, initials, coins, achievements, daily challenges, unlocked themes and skins, and settings in your browser or app storage. The installed apps also keep a native copy of these saves on the device. These features do not require a Pixel Nook account.

Local saves are not automatically uploaded to a Pixel Nook cloud-save account. Your operating system may include app data in its backup or device-transfer features, depending on your settings.

SAVE / TRANSFER lets you export your progress or import a save. Exporting is your choice: the app opens the device's sharing options, and the destination you select may store a copy under its own privacy practices. Imports keep local recovery copies before merging progress.

Export before you uninstall. Removing an app, clearing browser/app data, or changing devices can remove local saves. Keep an exported copy somewhere you control. Installing the app does not automatically read saves from Safari or Chrome.

03 / The public leaderboard

When you choose SUBMIT, the game sends your selected three-character initials, game and score to our leaderboard. You can choose SKIP and continue playing. Submitted initials and scores may be displayed publicly to other players.

The leaderboard also records the submission time and an IP-derived identifier to limit spam and abuse. The game database stores a hash of the IP address rather than the raw IP address. This is a pseudonymous identifier; hashing does not make it anonymous.

Viewing the website or online leaderboard makes network requests to our hosting service. Connection information, including an IP address, is processed to deliver and protect those requests. This happens even when you do not submit a score.

We use Vercel for hosting and the score API, and Neon for the leaderboard database. Our game database does not store a legal name, email address or password with a score. Three-character initials are not a unique or verified identity.

04 / Ads and consent in the apps

Where advertising is enabled, the installed apps use Google AdMob to show ads between completed games. Google supplies the ads and uses its User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage applicable consent messages and privacy choices.

These services may process device or advertising identifiers, approximate location derived from network information, ad views and interactions, app interactions, and diagnostic or performance information. Uses include delivering and measuring advertising, analytics, consent management, and preventing fraud or abuse. Depending on your choices and the applicable configuration, advertising may be personalized.

Consent information is checked before the app initializes and loads ads. The consent check itself communicates with Google, so the absence of an ad does not mean no network request has occurred. On iOS, access to tracking identifiers is also subject to Apple's tracking permission.

Open AD PRIVACY in the installed app to review available consent options. You can also use your device's advertising and tracking settings. Available options depend on your region and device. The website does not initialize these native in-app advertising SDKs.

For further details, see how Google uses information from apps that use its services, Google's iOS SDK data disclosures, and Google's Android SDK data disclosures.

05 / Support, providers and security

If you email us, we receive the email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use that information to respond to your request and investigate the issue. Please do not send passwords, payment details or other information that is not needed for support.

We use service providers to host Pixel Nook, store leaderboard records, handle communications and supply advertising/consent services. Those providers process information needed for their services. Their processing may take place in countries other than yours.

The game's leaderboard connections use HTTPS. We keep database credentials out of the shipped game. No transmission or storage method is an absolute guarantee of security.

Provider information is available in the Vercel privacy notice and Neon's provider privacy notice (Databricks), as well as Google's information linked above.

06 / Retention and requests

Local saves and import recovery copies remain in browser/app storage until that data is removed or affected by device or browser storage management. Files you export and any OS backups are separate copies.

Submitted leaderboard records currently have no automatic expiry. They are retained to maintain the shared standings and help prevent abuse. The rate-limit windows do not delete records. Installing or updating the app does not reset the public leaderboard.

Support messages, provider logs, backups and advertising/consent data may have different retention practices. Their retention depends on the service, its settings and applicable requirements; there is not one fixed period for every category.

You can email kingdom@codecastle.ai to ask about your data or request access, correction, deletion or other rights that may apply. For a leaderboard request, include the game, initials, score and approximate submission date. We may need further information to identify the relevant record and assess the request; matching initials alone do not establish ownership.

Clearing local storage or uninstalling does not remove scores already submitted to the public leaderboard. Export any progress you want to keep before changing local storage. Contact us about public records rather than assuming a local reset affects them.

07 / Changes and help

We may update this policy as Pixel Nook or its services change. The date at the top identifies the current version. Questions about the game, saved progress or an ad can also be sent through the contact details on our support page.
