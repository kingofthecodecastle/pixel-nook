01 / Keep your progress safe

Before clearing data or reinstalling, export your save. Open SAVE / TRANSFER → EXPORT SAVE and keep the file somewhere you control. Clearing app or browser storage can remove local progress.

If the game reports a backup problem, export first. Do not uninstall as the first troubleshooting step. Import recovery copies are available under SAVE / TRANSFER → Recovery copies, when an import has created them.

02 / Common questions

How do I move my browser progress into the app?

Open Pixel Nook in the original browser and profile. Choose SAVE / TRANSFER → EXPORT SAVE. In the installed app, open SAVE / TRANSFER, select the file or paste its text, then choose IMPORT & RELOAD.

Import merges scores and unlocked progress with what is already on the device. Coin balances use the higher value instead of adding both balances. Existing initials and selected settings stay selected. Keep the exported file until you have checked the result.

Why is the new app showing different local scores?

The app and your web browser use separate storage. Installing the app cannot automatically read Safari or Chrome saves. Use export/import to bring them across. A different browser, profile or device also has its own local save.

What is the difference between local and global highscores?

Local scores are stored on your device. The online leaderboard is shared with other players. Choosing SUBMIT sends your initials and score to that public board; SKIP lets you keep playing without submitting.

If the online service is unavailable, the game uses local scores. A score shown only locally has not necessarily reached the public leaderboard. Export/import moves local progress; it does not publish old scores to the global board.

What should I do if an ad does not close or looks inappropriate?

Use the ad's own close, skip or report controls when available. Ad duration and those controls are supplied by the ad provider. If something looks wrong, email us the approximate time, device, game and advertiser shown. A screenshot is helpful if you can take one safely.

Do not clear your saves to resolve an ad problem. You can review available consent options with AD PRIVACY in the installed app and use your device's advertising/tracking settings. Read the advertising privacy information.

Does Pixel Nook need an account?

No Pixel Nook account is needed to play. Your three-character leaderboard initials are not a unique account or a sign-in. Keep exported saves if you want to move or safeguard local progress.

Can I ask about a public score or my data?

Yes. Email kingdom@codecastle.ai. For a leaderboard record, include the game, initials, score and approximate date. We may need more information to identify the relevant entry. Removing local app data does not remove a score already submitted publicly.

03 / Tell us what happened

For a useful bug report, include:

  • The cartridge name and what you were doing.
  • Whether you used the website, iOS app or Android app.
  • Your device and operating-system version; app version if available.
  • What you expected, what happened, and any visible error message.

Send only information you are comfortable sharing. Do not send passwords or payment details. A full save file is not needed for an initial report; keep a private backup while we investigate.
