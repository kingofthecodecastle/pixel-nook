## Review notes — English, ready to paste with the matching release

```text
Pixel Nook is a collection of twelve playable arcade games. Game code, graphics and audio generation are bundled with the app. No login, subscription or purchase is required to access any game. Coins are earned through play and spent on cosmetic themes and skins; they are not bought for money or redeemable for money.

To review:
1. Select any cartridge from the cabinet. Tap the game screen or use the on-screen controls to start. INFO explains the rules and controls for that cartridge.
2. In Snake, starting and continuing straight into the wall produces Game Over. After the short input grace, RESTART opens a new ready screen; tapping the game screen after Game Over starts a new round.
3. A prepared interstitial may appear when starting another round after Game Over. There is no interstitial on the first launch or during active play. Frequency spacing applies, and no-fill/offline conditions let play continue. Close and skip controls belong to the native advertising SDK. Missing an ad during a review attempt does not prevent testing the games.
4. AD PRIVACY in the cabinet opens available advertising privacy options. Consent UI pauses gameplay. An advertising/tracking consent decision is not required to unlock a cartridge.
5. The leaderboard uses an HTTPS service. Three-character initials and scores are public. Without a connection, the app shows local scores instead. Pixel Paddle and Tank Duel submit single-player scores only.
6. SAVE / TRANSFER exports progress through the device share sheet and imports save JSON. Import merges with existing progress and keeps recovery copies. Browser saves must be exported from their original browser/profile before importing into the app; they are not silently read from Safari or Chrome.
7. Camera, CRT, sound, themes, skins, achievements and the daily challenge are available in the app. Local two-player modes in Pixel Paddle and Tank Duel use compatible controllers or a keyboard; single-player touch controls remain available.

There is no remote downloadable game-code system and no account-based cloud save. The English interface is the same in all store localizations. Support and privacy-policy links are supplied in the store's corresponding fields.
```

## Content-rating and privacy evidence for completing forms

Use the questionnaires' actual categories and the final SDK configuration; do not preselect an age rating from this document. Apple calculates ratings from the supplied declarations. [Apple age-rating setup](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-app-information/set-an-app-age-rating/).

- The games include stylized space shooting, tank shots, crashing vehicles, ghosts and barrels. There is no realistic gore or graphic violence in the authored game content.
- No real-money wagering, loot boxes, cash-out, paid coin purchase, subscription or account creation is implemented. The leaderboard is score-based and does not award real-world prizes.
- Players choose three public alphanumeric initials; the API filters some offensive combinations. Declare this constrained player-submitted content accurately rather than treating the leaderboard as a private local-only feature.
- Local scores, coins, unlocks and settings use device storage and native backups. Save export occurs only when requested and shares a file through the device's chosen destination.
- The score API receives submitted game ID, initials and score; the database also stores a hashed IP value for abuse limiting and a timestamp. Hosting services may process network metadata. Do not describe the release as collecting no data.
- Google Mobile Ads/UMP are integrated third-party SDKs. App Privacy and Data safety must include their actual data handling, consent and tracking configuration. A general-ad-content-rating setting does not establish that the app is a children's app or that all Families/Kids requirements are satisfied.
- Review the app's real support/reporting route for inappropriate advertisements and any public-score complaint before final submission. Do not claim a support route is operational until its actual page/contact is verified.
